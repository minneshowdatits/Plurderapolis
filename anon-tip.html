Anonymous Tip Line

See something sketchy at a show? Report it here. No name, no email, no account.

How it works

Use this form to report safety concerns, harassment, or anything sketchy happening in the scene. Tips go straight to the PLURderapolis crew so we can follow up, warn the community, or adjust our booth setup.

How anonymous is this, honestly?

We don't ask for your name, email, phone number, or any account. The form doesn't even have fields for them, so we never see that info.

What we can't promise: this page loads standard analytics and your tip passes through a third-party form service, so network-level data (like an IP address) touches systems outside our control. We don't log it, store it, or try to trace it. All we ever receive is the words you type.

If you want stronger cover, submit over a VPN or Tor Browser.

What to report

  • Safety concerns at events
  • Harassment or consent violations
  • Substance-related issues
  • Theft
  • Event-specific issues
  • Anything else affecting the community
