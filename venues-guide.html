Venue groups to know

A few operators run multiple rooms. Their house rules and access contacts are shared, so it helps to know who is who.

The First Avenue family

  • First Avenue Mainroom
  • 7th St Entry
  • Fine Line
  • Turf Club
  • Palace Theatre (co-operated)
  • Fitzgerald Theater

Shared to know: one ADA contact for all rooms — info@first-avenue.com or (612) 338-8388, at least 24 hours before the show (by 5pm Friday for weekend shows). At 18+ shows, under-18s get in only with their own parent or legal guardian plus proof of guardianship.

The Skyway complex

  • Skyway Theatre (Mainroom)
  • The Loft
  • Studio B

Shared to know: all three rooms share one building and one FAQ at 711 Hennepin Ave, Minneapolis. For accessibility, call the box office at 612-333-6100 in advance so staff can set things up. No backpacks anywhere in the complex.

Recurring event series, not a venue

Takeover Thursdays (Electro Abi's) — weekly bass music night, 18+, at Abi's Restaurant, 2828 Lyndale Ave S, Minneapolis. Doors 9pm; vendors and art, kitchen open late. electroabis.com

Safety basics

Written for you in the crowd, not for promoters.

In a crowd

If you feel unsafe or overwhelmed, move toward the edge of the crowd where there is more space and air. If you fall, curl up to protect your head and neck and get back on your feet as soon as you can. Keep your phone and ID on your body, not in a loose bag.

Finding help

Look for marked medical or first-aid stations, or ask any staff member or security — pointing you to medics is their job. You do not need a perfect explanation; “my friend needs help over here” is enough.

If a friend needs help

Stay with them. Get staff or security right away and describe what is happening: what they took (if you know), when, and what symptoms you see. Stay until help arrives — do not leave someone alone to “sleep it off.”

When to call 911

Call 911 if someone is unresponsive, not breathing normally, having a seizure, or you are unsure. Minnesota's Good Samaritan law (604A.05) protects people who seek help during an overdose. What to do in an emergency

Getting home safe

Ride Safety & Bystander Basics

The ride home is part of the night. Our full guide covers rideshares, walking, and stepping in when something looks off.

  • Verify your pickup spot before you leave the venue — check the venue listing above for its recommended rideshare area.
  • Match the car, plate, and driver to your app before you get in. If anything does not line up, cancel and rebook.
  • Share your trip with a friend so someone can follow along in real time.
  • Sit in the back seat, keep your belongings with you, and trust your gut — you can always end the ride.
