New here? Start here.

Plurderapolis is a Minnesota harm reduction crew, run by ravers for ravers. If you are new to the scene or new to us, this is the five minute version of everything we teach. No lectures, just the stuff that keeps people safe.

The five basics ▾
🧪

Test your stuff

Fentanyl and xylazine test strips are cheap, legal in Minnesota, and take two minutes.

How to use test strips →
💧

Drink water, eat food

Dehydration and low blood sugar ruin more nights than anything else. Set a reminder and actually eat.

Raver health guide →
👥

Buddy system

Go with someone you trust, check in on each other through the night, and pick a meetup spot in case phones die.

Buddy check timers →
💬

Consent

Only yes means yes. Check in, respect no, and look out for each other on the floor.

Consent basics →
🆘

Know the overdose signs

Unresponsive, slow or no breathing, blue lips or fingertips. Call 911 and give naloxone if you have it.
