Cravings SOS

Ride the Wave

Five steps, about a minute each. Start it and just follow along.

Step 1 of 5

Name it

1:00

You rode the whole wave. How is the craving now?

A craving is a wave. It peaks, then it fades, usually within 20 minutes. You just have to ride this one out. Here is five minutes:

  1. Name it. Say it in your head: "This is a craving, not a command." Naming it puts you back in charge.
  2. Breathe for one minute. Hit start below and follow the circle. Slow breathing tells your nervous system the emergency is over.
  3. Move your state. Cold water on your face, step outside for air, find your buddy. Cravings hate a change of scenery.
  4. Play it forward. Picture tomorrow morning: waking up clear, no shame spiral, no mystery charges. That version of you is five minutes away.
  5. Tell someone. Text your buddy, find the sober meetup, or just say it out loud. Cravings get quieter when they are not a secret.
Ready

If the craving will not quit, that is data, not failure. Reach out here — support groups, crisis lines, and people who get it.

Mocktail Recipes

Mocktail builder

Pick a mood, tick what is already in your kitchen, and get the closest recipe from this page, plus what you still need. Ice is a given.

Flavor mood

Already in your kitchen

Electric Lemonade
  1. Fill a glass with ice
  2. Pour 6 oz lemonade
  3. Add 1 oz non-alcoholic blue curaçao syrup
  4. Top with 2 oz sparkling water
  5. Stir gently
  6. Garnish with a lemon slice

Bonus: glows under blacklight!

Ginger Citrus Fizz
  1. Fill a glass with ice
  2. Add 4 oz ginger beer
  3. Squeeze 1 oz fresh lime juice
  4. Add 1 oz fresh orange juice
  5. Drop in a few fresh mint leaves
  6. Stir lightly and garnish with an orange wedge
Virgin Mojito
  1. Muddle 8 fresh mint leaves with 1 tsp sugar in a glass
  2. Add juice of 1 lime
  3. Fill the glass with ice
  4. Top with soda water
  5. Stir well and garnish with a mint sprig and lime wedge
PLUR Punch
  1. Mix 4 oz cranberry juice with 2 oz pineapple juice
  2. Add 1 oz fresh lime juice
  3. Fill with ice and top with club soda
  4. Garnish with lime wheel and edible glitter

Perfect for rave-themed gatherings!
