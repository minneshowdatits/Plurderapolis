For the friend

Helping someone who is panicking?

Do this

  • Stay with them. Your calm presence is the main tool.
  • Speak slowly and simply: "You are safe. I am staying right here."
  • Breathe with them so they have slow breaths to copy.
  • Offer small choices: "Want water? Want to step outside?"
  • Ask gently what they took and when, in case medics need to know.

Do not do this

  • Do not say "calm down" or "you are fine." It never helps.
  • Do not restrain them or crowd them. Give them space.
  • Do not leave them alone to ride it out.

Get medics when

  • Crushing chest pain, pain spreading to the arm or jaw, or fainting.
  • They stop responding to you, or their breathing looks wrong.
  • The panic has not eased after 20 to 30 minutes.
  • You are unsure about anything. Calling is always the right call.

In danger, or panic will not stop?

Get a real person involved. That is always the right call.

    🍑 PLURderapolis: Panic Pocket Card

    You are safe. This will pass.

    Breathe: 4 - 4 - 6

    • Breathe in 4 seconds, hold 4, breathe out 6. Repeat until your heart slows.

    Ground: 5-4-3-2-1

    • 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste.

    Get a person

    • Call 911 if you are in danger. Call or text 988 anytime.
    • Text HOME to 741741. MN Warmline: 651-288-0400.
    • Show someone nearby: "Please stay with me or help me get to safety."
