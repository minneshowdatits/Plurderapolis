Get the PLURderapolis App

Harm Reduction for Minnesota Ravers, one tap from your home screen when you need it most.

Why add it?

🍑 Your own icon Plurderapolis sits on your home screen like any other app. No searching, no typing the address in a panic.
📱 Opens fullscreen It opens on its own, without the browser bars, so the guides and tools fill your whole screen.
📶 Works with no signal The crisis pages are saved on your phone, so they still open in a basement, a warehouse, or a field with no signal.

Good to know: there is no store download and no account to make. This is the website saved to your home screen. It is free, it takes about ten seconds, and you can remove it any time like any other app.

On iPhone

  1. Open plurderapolis.org in Safari. It has to be Safari for this to work.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen. It looks like a square with an arrow pointing up.
  3. Scroll down in the menu and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add in the top corner. The peach icon will appear on your home screen.

Tip: open it once while you still have signal, so the crisis pages can save to your phone before you head out.

On Android

  1. Open plurderapolis.org in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu button, the three dots in the top corner.
  3. Tap Install app. On some phones it says Add to Home Screen instead.
  4. Tap Install to confirm. The peach icon will appear on your home screen and in your app list.

Tip: Chrome may also offer to install it on its own after you have visited a couple of times. Saying yes does the same thing.

On a computer

Using Chrome on a computer? Open plurderapolis.org, then look for the install icon in the address bar, or open the Chrome menu and choose the install option. It will open in its own window, like a desktop app.

Do not see the option?

Check which browser you are in first. On iPhone it only works from Safari, and on Android it works best from Chrome. If you opened the link inside another app, copy plurderapolis.org into Safari or Chrome and try again from the homepage.
