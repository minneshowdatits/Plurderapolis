Harm Reduction & Overdose Prevention

  • Minneapolis NARCAN Vending Machines
    Free, anonymous, 24-hour NARCAN (naloxone) outside four Minneapolis fire stations, no questions asked. NEW: Fire Station 12, 5401 33rd Ave S (the city's fourth machine). Also Fire Station 5 (2700 Bloomington Ave S), Fire Station 14 (2002 Lowry Ave N), and Fire Station 21 (3209 38th St E). Machines are restocked regularly; each box contains two doses.
    Minneapolis, MN
  • Minnesota Overdose Trends: 2025 Data (MDH)
    Minnesota Department of Health, October 27, 2026: opioid overdose deaths fell from 1,039 in 2022 to 671 in 2025, and fentanyl involved deaths fell from 602 to 564. But deaths from all drugs rose from 994 in 2024 to 1,025 in 2025, driven by stimulants. Naloxone reverses opioid overdoses; it does not counteract stimulants.
    Minnesota
  • Steve Rummler HOPE Network
