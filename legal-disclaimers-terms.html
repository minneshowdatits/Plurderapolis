3. Emergency & Immediate Help

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, experiencing a medical emergency, or suspected drug overdose, call 911 immediately.

Other Minnesota-specific emergency contacts:

  • Poison Help / Minnesota Regional Poison Center: 1‑800‑222‑1222 (24/7)
  • Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (988): Call or text 988, or chat online at 988lifeline.org
  • Naloxone access: Minnesota supports naloxone distribution and administration under state standing orders. Administer naloxone if trained and call 911 immediately.
  • Good Samaritan law: Minnesota's overdose Good Samaritan law, Minn. Stat. 604A.05, protects people who seek medical help during a drug overdose from certain drug possession charges. Naloxone access and administration are covered by Minn. Stat. 604A.04.

These resources are not substitutes for emergency services, but they are state-supported channels to protect health and safety.
