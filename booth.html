What Our Safety Booths Provide

Comprehensive harm reduction services for Minneapolis, St. Paul music festivals, EDM events, and raves

💊

Overdose Prevention

We provide free naloxone (Narcan) kits, fentanyl test strips, and overdose response training to prevent tragedies at Twin Cities events.

💧

Hydration Support

Water stations, electrolyte supplements, and cooling areas to prevent dehydration and heat-related issues at Minnesota festivals.

🛋️

Safe Spaces

Chill-out areas with seating, ear protection, and calming resources for attendees feeling overwhelmed at EDM events.

📚

Education & Resources

Honest information about substance safety, consent culture, mental health resources, and community care for rave attendees.

🛡️

Safety Resources

Basic first aid, safe escorts to vehicles, and connections to medical or additional support services when needed at music events.

❤️

Peer Support

Trained volunteers offering non-judgmental support, active listening, and de-escalation techniques for festival-goers.

About PLURderapolis

Driven by community need in the Twin Cities music scene

Since 2019, PLURderapolis has been providing essential harm reduction services at Minneapolis, St. Paul, and Twin Cities electronic music events and EDM festivals. We believe that everyone deserves to enjoy music events safely, with dignity, and without judgment.

Our trained volunteers create safer spaces where party-goers can access resources, education, and support at Minnesota raves and festivals. We meet people where they're at, offering practical tools for risk reduction while fostering a culture of care and community responsibility in the Twin Cities music scene.
